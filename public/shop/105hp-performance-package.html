105hp Performance Package

From £3860.00 (inc VAT)

A brand new package from Specialist Components.

Featuring our renown EFI throttle body kit with fully mappable ignition, this is a combination that simply works. We have packaged our kit with the AC Dodd RS+ camshaft, oil pump, new race quality cam followers (with oil drain hole) and duplex timing kit. This kit has the addition of our successful Superport cylinder head 2023 spec

The drivability from this combination means you have a smooth idle, plenty of torque from 1500 - 7000rpm, and instant throttle response. After numerous testing with a whole range of camshafts we feel that this is THE best performing fast road cam available (we have tested them all!).

The addition of the Superport cylinder head means you will achieve substantial airflow improvements given you more torque and power. Below are the figures this combination has performed:

1293cc - 95hp / 90lbft.
1380cc - 105hp / 101lbft

The bundle consists of:

SC full EFI throttle body kit (SPI, MPI or 'Carb')
SC Supersport 36*31 cylinder head
AC Dodd RS+ billet cross-drilled camshaft
Race quality cam followers with oil drainage hole
High capacity oil pump
Duplex timing kit with IWIS comp spec chain, alloy and steel construction for lightweight and durability.

The Delta 400 ECU will come with a calibration to suit the cam and head meaning you will be able to get your car up and running once everything is fitted.

Please note - The throttle body assembly as standard will come supplied with our billet ram pipe and ITG air filter. If you require an inlet stub as opposed to this, please contact us before ordering.
