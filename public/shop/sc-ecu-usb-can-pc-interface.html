What's in the box?

The USB-CAN interface with 1 m of cable and a USB connector.

Which ECUs does it work with?

It's designed to connect your PC to Specialist Components ECUs. If you're not sure about your setup, give us a call and we'll check for you.

Can I change settings while the engine is running?

Yes. You can view live data, change parameters in real time, and then save them to the ECU or to disk.

How do I know it's communicating with the ECU?

There are two micro LEDs on the rear of the unit. The red LED flashes while it's talking to the ECU.
