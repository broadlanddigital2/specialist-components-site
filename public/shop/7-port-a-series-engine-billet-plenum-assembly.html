7 Port A-Series Engine Billet Plenum Assembly

From £1350.00 (inc VAT)

Simply stunning new product for 2024, Compact billet plenum including incorporated fuel rail for the cast alloy 7 port cylinder head. Using decades of our experience with the A-Series engines and turbo engines has resulted in the creation of this amazing plenum. Tapered inlet runners from the head face to the machined ram pipe, over 2.25L capacity, pico injectors for ultra compact design firing direct into the airstream of the ports, 5 axis milled throughout, "O" ring sealing to cylinder head, supplied with all fixings and "O" rings for assembly. The plenum comes assembled with a 55mm "shaftless"/sealed AT Power billet throttle body. Discrete mounted 2 x 6mm "vac" stubs for boost reference on MAP sensors/gauges, includes intergrated fuel rail with AN-6 fittings.
