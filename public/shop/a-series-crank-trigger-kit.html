A Series Crank Trigger Kit

From £185.00 (inc VAT)

Specialist Components' own design of crankshaft trigger wheel and crankshaft sensor mounting kit that is a “bolt on fit” to the A+ engine.
The ignition trigger wheel is 36-1 tooth configuration, compatible with most engine management systems including our own. The trigger wheel is precision laser cut and mounted to a lightweight high grade steel boss-plated silver for corrosion resistance. The sensor mounting bracket is of billet alloy construction for light weight and strength and has full adjustment built into it for accurate sensor positioning.
Billet parts are supplied anodised silver.
The kit includes an extended crank bolt to allow for the additional thickness of the mounting boss.

We use a SEN8D sensor and this is optionally included with the kit.
Please contact us if you have any questions.
