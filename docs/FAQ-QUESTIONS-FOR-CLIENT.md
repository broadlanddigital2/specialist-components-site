# FAQ questions that need answers from Specialist Components

The category FAQs on the demo only use facts the site already states:
- what's in each range
- price ranges, inc VAT
- where stock is shown
- technical help
- worldwide express delivery
- contact details and opening hours

Customers will also ask the questions below. Only the business can answer them, so they're not on the site yet. Once you send answers, they can go into the FAQs on the right pages.

## Every range
1. What's your returns policy, and how long do customers have?
2. What warranty or guarantee do your parts carry?
3. What are typical lead times for out-of-stock or made-to-order items?
4. How much does UK delivery cost and how long does it take? Is there a free delivery threshold?
5. Which countries do you ship to, and how is international delivery priced (including duties and taxes)?
6. Do you offer trade or club accounts, or bulk pricing?
7. Can customers collect from the workshop in Carlton Rode?
8. Do you offer a fitting, installation or setup service?

## Complete Crate Engines, TwinKam engines and Rotrex engines
9. What's included with a crate engine? For example, is it dyno-tested, with ancillaries, or with an ECU?
10. Are engines built to order? How long is a typical build?
11. Is there a running-in procedure or warranty condition customers must follow?
12. Can a customer's own engine be rebuilt or upgraded instead?

## TwinKam 16v Conversion
13. Which Minis or subframes does the conversion suit?
14. What else is needed to complete a conversion? For example, gearbox, cooling or wiring.
15. Is the build guide included with the kits, or sold separately?

## Engine Management (including MGB and Porsche)
16. Which engines and vehicles is the engine management suitable for?
17. Do you supply ECUs pre-mapped, or offer a mapping service?
18. Is ECU software included, and which operating systems does it run on?

## Classic Mini Split Rim Wheels
19. Which sizes, widths and offsets are available, and which cars do they fit?
20. Are the wheels supplied assembled? Do they need specific tyres or wheel nuts?

## Cylinder heads, A-Series components and performance packages
21. Are the heads new or reconditioned? Are they suitable for unleaded fuel?
22. What power gains can customers expect from the performance packages?
