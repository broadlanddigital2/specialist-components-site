Twin Kam Conversion Bundle Package

From £5600.00 (inc VAT)

The conversion kit is just the start of the comprehensive range of ‘K’ conversion parts and accessories offered by Specialist Components.
For those who want everything in one package SC are launching their complete Twin Kam Conversion Package. It includes everything needed to build an 8V or 16V ‘K’ Twin Kam at a special bundle deal price.
What you get:
SC ‘K’ Conversion Kit
SC 4:2:1 Stainless T304 manifold - Road
SC thermostat for the K
SC Bespoke Bi-metallic Head Gasket
Refurbishing, Machining and Assembly of your ‘K’ Cylinder Head
SC D400 Engine Management System pre-mapped for your engine
SC Bespoke ‘K’ Wiring Loom, Ignition Coil Pack, HT leads, Coil Bracket and sensors
SC Ancillaries Kit (including Throttle Cable and Dipstick)
SC Crank Trigger Kit with Crank Position Sensor
Preparation of your A-series block for the ‘K’ head
De-grease, surface, re-bore, hone and centre main strap machining of your block
New core plugs for oil gallery + water after block has been rod cleaned out.
Pocketing of your pistons for valve cut-outs

Please select the type of head and whether or not you will be supplying a donor cylinder head.
Please note - this package does not include supply of pistons or engine block
